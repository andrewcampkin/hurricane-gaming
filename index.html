Chalk

Our CrossFit logbook for Android, and it's open source.

Log what you actually did

The class WOD, a heavy single, an EMOM you made up. Chalk records any of it in a handful of taps and shows your records and your recent history for any movement at a glance.

  • Everything stays on the phone: no account, no server, works with no signal.
  • Personal records derived automatically, surfaced the moment they happen.
  • Movement vocabulary and benchmark WODs preloaded. Logging Fran finds every Fran.
  • JSON backup and restore from Settings.

git-for-ai

Source control that remembers why. Open source, for anyone whose code is written by AI agents.

Ask your repo why

git-for-ai layers an intent ledger, AI-agent session capture and a local semantic index on top of the Git you already use. Six months on, you or your agent can ask why a line is the way it is and get the actual reasoning back, not just a commit SHA and a name.

  • Claude Code sessions are captured at commit time, redacted, and stored as ordinary git notes and refs.
  • A local review page shows each commit's diff beside the intent that was recorded for it.
  • Ask a question in plain English and get a cited answer drawn from code, intent and sessions.
  • Nothing leaves your machine until you push it. Uninstall the tool and the repo is still a normal git repo.
